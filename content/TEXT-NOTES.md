# Notes on the text

For the scholar who reviews this site. Everything here is about the Sanskrit text itself, not the meanings.

## Sources

- **Master text** (names, verse layout, archana forms): the Lalitā Sahasranāma stotram and nāmāvali on stotranidhi.com.
- **Checked against**: *Śrīlalitāsahasranāmastotram (Saubhāgyabhāskaravyākhyāsahitam)*, the edition of Bhāskararāya's commentary on Sanskrit Wikisource.
- The 1000 names were matched to their verses by program. 977 matched automatically; the other 23 (names joined by sandhi, or archana forms that differ from the verse form) were placed by reading the verse.

## Variant readings noted in the master text

- Verses 29–30, names 75–76: some editions swap *Viṣaṅga* and *Viśukra* (who slays whom). The site follows *Mantriṇyambāviracitaviṣaṅgavadhatoṣitā* (75) and *Viśukraprāṇaharaṇavārāhīvīryananditā* (76).
- Verse 111, name 547: *Barbarālakā*; some editions read *Bandhurālakā*.

Spelling mistakes in the web list:

- 82 सभाण्डासुर… → सभण्डासुर…
- 603 गुरूमूर्त्यै → गुरुमूर्त्यै
- 680 भावाभावविर्जितायै → भावाभावविवर्जितायै
- 697 सर्वलोकवंशकर्यै → सर्वलोकवशङ्कर्यै
- 933 मङ्गलाकृत्ये → मङ्गलाकृत्यै
- 963 शामात्मिकायै → शमात्मिकायै (the verse and Bhāskararāya read शम)

Grammar of the dative form (please confirm):

- 306 राज्ञै → राज्ञ्यै
- 550 वियदादिजगत्प्रसवे → वियदादिजगत्प्रसुवे; 837 वियत्प्रसुवे uses the same form. Both are declined like the -भू names, and -प्रस्वै may be the better form.
- 779 विरजायै → विरजसे: Bhāskararāya reads the name as विरजाः (an s-stem).
- 795 कामदुघे → कामदुहे
- 855 गगनान्तःस्थायै → गगनान्तस्थायै, to match the verse spelling.

Each name file's `review.notes` records why its form was changed, along with the other points a scholar should check.
